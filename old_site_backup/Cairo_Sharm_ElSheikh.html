Cairo and Sharm El Sheikh

Day 1:

Meet and assist upon arrival to Cairo Airport
Transfer by private car to your Hotel for check in and overnight

Day 2:
Breakfast at Hotel. Visit to the Giza plateau and the Pyramids of Cheops, Chephren
and Mycrenos. Visit to Sphinx. Lunch in local restaurant. Visit to Sakkara, (Step pyramid).
Overnight in Hotel
                       
Day 3:
Breakfast in Hotel and check out. Visit to Egyptian Museum, the Citadel of Salah El Din, Mohamed Ali Mosque. Then lunch in local restaurant. Visit the Shopping area of Khan El Khalili Bazaar before heading to Cairo Airport for departure to Sharm El Sheikh.
Meet and assist at Sharm El Sheikh Airport. Transfer to your Hotel for check in and overnight.

From Day 4 to Day 9:
Spend the next week taking pleasure in tanning, swimming, relaxing, shopping and enjoying Sharm’s night life. Or you could choose one or all of the activities listed below organized by our office in Sharm El Sheikh:

  1. Camel Safari
  2. Over day in Saint Catherine to climb Mount Sinai and visit Saint Catherine Monastery
  3. Shopping and snorkelling in Dahab
  4. 4x4 Safari to the Colored Canyon
  5. Over day in Luxor to visit the Valley of the Kings
  6. Sea Safari in Ras Mohamed

Day 10:

Breakfast in Hotel. Then transfer to Sharm International Airport for final departure.
