Cairo and Sinai

Day 01:           
Arrive Cairo international airport, Meet and assist by our representative through customs and immigration formalities and transfer to Hotel for overnight

Day 02:           
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day guided tour to Giza Pyramids with Sphinx, and Sakkara, lunch during sightseeing then transfer back to the hotel.
Overnight at hotel in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 03:           
Full day city tour visiting: The Egyptian Museum, The Citadel, Mohamed Ali Mosque, the Old Coptic Churches in Old Cairo "Abou Serga and the Hanging Church and Khan El Khalili  Bazaars.
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch.

Day 04:           
A.M. Over-day to Alexandria by car visiting: Alexandria National Museum, the Roman Amphitheatre, the Catacombs, Alexandria Bibliotheca and from outside view to the "Pompey's Pillar and the Qait-bay Fortress".
Sea food Lunch at local Restaurant over-looking the Mediterranean Sea...
Overnight at hotel in Cairo.
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch.

Day 05:           
A.M.Check out and transfer to St. Catherine 390 KM, 5 hours drive passing by Suez Canal, Moses Springs, and Wady Firan en route.
Arrival St. Catherine, check-in, dinner and overnight.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and dinner.

Day 06:           
Optional early morning Climb Mt. Sinai to watch sunrise for volunteers only.
Check out from hotel after breakfast.
Visit St. Catherine Monastery.
Transfer by car to Dahab.
Overnight at hotel.
Meals: Breakfast and dinner.

From Day 07 to day 09:           
Enjoy the Sun, the sea and the desert
Overnight at hotel.
Meals: Breakfast and dinner.

Day 10:           
AM.Breakfast at hotel. Enjoy the Sun, the sea.
PM. Transfer to Cairo and check-in.                    
Overnight at hotel.
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch.

Day 11:           
Check out from hotel after breakfast.
Transfer to Cairo International Airport for final departure.

Meals: Breakfast
