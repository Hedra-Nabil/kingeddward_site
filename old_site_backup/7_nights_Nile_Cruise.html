Classical Egypt and 7 Nights Nile Cruise

Day 01:           
Arrive Cairo international airport, Meet and assist by our representative through customs and immigration formalities and transfer to Hotel for overnight

Day 02:           
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day guided tour to Giza Pyramids with Sphinx, and Sakkara, lunch during sightseeing then transfer back to the hotel.
Overnight at hotel in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 03:           
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day guided Tour to Egyptian Museum, citadel, Mohamed Ali mosque, and Khan El Khallili Bazaar, Lunch during tour
Transfer to Cairo APT for flight to Luxor
Meet and assist at Luxor APT and transfer to Nile Cruise
 Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Diner

Day 04:           
Tour to the west bank visiting Valley of the Kings, Temple of Hatshepsut at Deir El Bahari, and Colossi of Memnon
PM visit to Luxor and Karnak temples in the East bank
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Diner on board

Day 05:           
Sailing to Esna, cross the Lock, and Sail to Edfu
Overnight on board in Edfu
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Diner on board

Day 06:           
Morning visit to the Temple of Edfu, sail to Kom Ombo, visit the temple of Kom Ombo
Sail to Aswan, Overnight on board
Meals: Breakfast , Lunch and Diner on board

Day 07:           
AM free and possibility to visit Abu Simbel
PM Felucca ride
Meals: Breakfast , Lunch and Diner on board

Day 08:           
AM Visit to the High Dam, temple of Philae, and unfinished obelisk
Sail to Kom Ombo, sail to Edfu , overnight on board
 Meals: Breakfast , Lunch and Diner on board

Day 09:           
Sail to Esna, Cross the Lock, sail to Luxor for overnight
Meals: Breakfast , Lunch and Diner on board

Day 10:           
Disembarkation after breakfast and transfer to Luxor APT for flight to Cairo
Meet and assist at Cairo APT, transfer to Hotel and overnight in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast

Day 11:           
Check out after breakfast and transfer to APT for final departure

Meals: Breakfast
