Sonesta Sun Goddess Nile Cruise

On the Sonesta Sun Goddess Cruise Ship, enjoy an authentic Nile adventure that create memories to last a lifetime. Cruise the Nile for four or six nights from Aswan to Luxor, as the Sun Goddess travels from the Valley of Kings, to the mummified crocodiles of Kom Ombo.

This elegant Nile cruise ship features cabins with broad panoramic views, bathrooms with full-size bathtubs and all modern conveniences. Enjoy a swim in the outdoor pool, experience the exciting nightlife in the ship’s discotheque or a relaxing massage treatment at the spa. Deluxe amenities on the Sun Goddess Cruise Ship are delivered with the uncompromising personal service that is unmistakably Sonesta.
