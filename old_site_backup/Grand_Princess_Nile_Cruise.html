Technical data of M/S Grand Princess:-

Voltage: 220V
Total no of cabins: 66 Junior suites + 2 Royal suites
Boat Crew: 100
Length: 72 meters
Width: 14.5 meters
Height: 11.20
Draught: 1.6 meter
Main engine: 3 main engines (MAN) 500 H/P each
Generators: 3 generators (MAN) 280 K/W each
Fire system: electric fire, alarm system and sprinkling system
Water: UV (ultra violet system) for water sterilization
