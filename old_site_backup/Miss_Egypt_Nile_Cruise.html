Miss Egypt Nile Cruise

M/S Miss Egypt is a five-star deluxe floating hotel, built and constructed under the supervision of ABS (American Bureau and Shipping).

Decks:-

It consists of 5 Decks as follows:
Lower Deck (A)
• Engine room
• Galley
• Restaurant
• Staff cabins
• Laundry
Main Deck (B)
• 22 Junior suites
• Lobby
• Bazaar
Upper Deck (C)
• 2 Royal suites
• 26 Junior suites
• Piano bar
• Coiffeur
Lounge Deck (D)
• 18 Junior suites
• Lounge bar

Sun Deck (E)

Technical data of Miss Egypt :-

Voltage: 220V
Total no of cabins: 66 Junior suites + 2 Royal suites
Boat Crew: 100
Length: 72 meters
Width: 14.5 meters
Height: 11.50
Draught: 1.45 meter
Main engine: 3 Main Engines (MAN) 420 H/P
Generators: 3 Generators (MAN) 240 K/W
Fire system: Electric Fire and Alarm System – sprinkler system
Water: UV (ultra violet system) for water sterilization
