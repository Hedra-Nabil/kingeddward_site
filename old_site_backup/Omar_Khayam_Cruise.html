Omar El Khayam (Lake Nasser Cruise)

Description

Come aboard the 5-star deluxe cruise ship Steigenberger Omar El Khayam, and embark on an unprecedented adventure that takes you through the ancient era of Egyptian civilization. Discover the secrets of the past at the comfort of your spacious cabin or suite, furnished with chic modern décor. A plethora of sightseeing options are available on and offshore in the timeless areas of Abu Simble and Aswan. At night, wind up by enjoying gourmet dishes prepared by the finest chefs at the main restaurant, or further indulging at the luxurious lounge bar.

The Steigenberger Omar El Khayam has lots of facilities onboard for relaxation and amusement; a large sundeck with pool, pool bar, Jacuzzi ,Health club, fully equiped spa and massage parlor.

Ship information :

Number of Cabins 80
Dimensions 113m Length, 17m width and 2.5m draft.

Cabin Information:

In order to enjoy the various breathtaking views of the Nile, the main and upper deck cabins have large Panoramic Window which opens into a balcony with seating area. All the cabins contain a private restroom, bathtub and hair dryer.

Take pleasure in chilling out at the comfort of your cabin. All cabins are fully air conditioned with individually controls thermostats, and are equipped with a mini-fridge.

Keep in touch with the outside world through the TV with satellite channels, internal telephone as well as a music system available in all cabins. In addition, international telephone and Fax sending as well as internet browsing against charge are available on board.

Location:

4 Nights / 5 Days - Aswan / Abu Simbel
Day 1

Aswan
Embarkation before lunch.  Enjoy your time free at leisure in Aswan. Dinner and overnight.
Day 2

Aswan
Enjoy your breakfast wile sailing to Kalabsha where you will visit Kalabsha Temple dedicated to the Nubian god Mandulis. Continue to Beit El Wali and visit the temple dedicated to the goddess Hathor then visit the beautiful Kiosk of Kertassi. Back to the ship and sail to Wadi el Seboua. Overnight on board.
Day 3

WADI EL SEBOUA  / AMADA
After breakfast you will visit Wadi El Seboua, then take a camel ride or walk to El Dakka and Mehraka. Back to the ship for lunch. You will then continue sailing to Amada. Visit Amada temple, Ed Deir and the Tomb of Pennut then return to the ship. Overnight in Amada
Day 4

KASR IBRIM / ABU SIMBEL
Early breakfast while sailing to Kasr Ibrim. View the fortress from the sun deck and continue sailing to Abu Simbel. Once in Abu Simbel you will marvel at the beautiful Abu Simbel Temples of the mighty king Ramses II and his beautiful wife Nefertari.
Overnight in Abu Simbel
Day 5

ABU SIMBEL                   
Check out after breakfast.
