Classical Egypt and 3 Nights Nile Cruise

Day 01:           
Arrive Cairo international airport, Meet and assist by our representative through customs and immigration formalities and transfer to Hotel for overnight

Day 02:           
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day guided tour to Giza Pyramids with Sphinx, and Sakkara, lunch during sightseeing then transfer back to the hotel.
Overnight at hotel in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 03:           
Check out hotel after breakfast and transfer to Cairo airport for flight to Aswan. Meet and assist upon arrival in Aswan Airport and transfer to the Nile Cruise for embarkation then visit the unfinished obelisk, high dam, temple of Philae, sailing to Kom Ombo, visit the temple of two gods Sobek and Harouris, Sail to Edfu
Overnight on Board in Edfu
 Meals: Breakfast , Lunch and Diner on board

Day 04:           
Morning visit to temple of Horus in Edfu, Sail to Esna, Cross the Lock, and sail to Luxor, overnight in Luxor
 Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner on board

Day 05:           
Visit the west bank including Valley of the Kings, Temple of Hatshepsut, and Colossi of Memnon,
PM visit Luxor and Karnak Temples in the East Bank
Overnight on board
Meals: Breakfast , Lunch and Dinner on board

Day 06:           
Disembarkation after breakfast and transfer to Luxor APT for flight to Cairo,
Meet and assist at Cairo APT, transfer to the hotel for check in then begin a full day tour to Egyptian Museum, citadel, Mohamed Ali mosque, and Khan El Khallili Bazaar
Transfer to hotel, check in and overnight
 Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 07:           
Check out after breakfast and transfer to APT for final departure 

Meals: Breakfast
