Technical data of M/S Royal Princess

Voltage: 220V
Length: 72 meters
Width: 14 meters
Height: 11
Draught: 1.50 meter
Main engine: 3 Main engines (CAT – 480 HP) connected with 3 propellers (Aqua Master)
Generators: Diesel Generators CAT – 280KW
Fire system: Electric fire alarm system with addressable panel and sprinkling system
Switch panel: Matching main switch panel
Water: UV (ultra violet system) for water sterilization, plus Sand and Carbon filter
