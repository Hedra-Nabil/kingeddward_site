Classical Egypt and 4 Nights Nile Cruise

Day 01:           
Arrive Cairo international airport, Meet and assist by our representative through customs and immigration formalities and transfer to Hotel for overnight

Day 02:           
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day guided tour to Giza Pyramids with Sphinx, and Sakkara, lunch during sightseeing then transfer back to the hotel.
Overnight at hotel in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 03:
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day tour to Egyptian Museum, citadel, Mohamed Ali mosque, and Khan El Khallili Bazaar. Transfer back to the hotel.
Overnight at hotel in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 03:           
Check out after breakfast
Transfer to Cairo airport for flight to Luxor. Meet and assist upon arrival at Luxor airport, Transfer to Nile Cruise, embarkation, visit Valley of the Kings, temple of Hatshepsut, Colossi of Memnon in the west bank, Lunch on board
PM visit to Luxor temple and Karnak Temple
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Diner on board

Day 04:           
Early morning Sail to Esna, cross Esna Lock, Sail to Edfu
Overnight on board in Edfu
  Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Diner

Day 05:           
Morning Visit to the Temple of Horus in Edfu
Sail to Kom Ombo, visit the temple of two gods Sobek and Haruris, sail to Aswan, overnight in Aswan
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Diner

Day 06:           
 AM free and possibility to visit Abu Simbel
 PM visit the high dam, unfinished obelisk, temple of Philae, felucca ride
 Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Diner

Day 08:
Breakfast on board, disembarkation and transfer to Aswan APT for Flight to Cairo for final departure.
