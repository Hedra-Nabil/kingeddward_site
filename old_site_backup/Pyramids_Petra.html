The Pyramids and Petra

Day 01:            Cairo
Arrive Cairo international airport, Meet and assist by our representative through customs and immigration formalities and transfer to Hotel for overnight

Day 02:            Cairo
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day guided tour to Giza Pyramids with Sphinx, and Sakkara, lunch during sightseeing then transfer back to the hotel.
Overnight at hotel in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 03:            Cairo  
Breakfast at hotel,
Full day tour to visit Old Cairo quarter including visits to hanging church, church of St. Sirga and the Synagogue,  then visit the Egyptian Museum  
 Lunch during tour
Overnight at hotel in Cairo

Day 04:            Cairo / Amman / Petra
Check out after breakfast and transfer to Cairo international airport
Flight to Amman
Meet and assist at Amman airport
Transfer to Petra, overnight at hotel in Petra

Day 05:            Petra
Breakfast at hotel,
Full day tour to the red rose city Petra, Jordan’s biggest attraction. Petra was established around the 6th century B.C. by the Nabataean Arabs, a nomadic tribe who settled in the area and founded a commercial empire. Upon arrival you will begin the trip on horse back to the Siq, the canyon, and then by foot until you reach the most beautiful monument: the Treasury. Among other monuments you will see are the Street of Facades, the Royal Tombs and Pharaoh's Castle. Overnight in Petra

Day 06:            Petra / Amman
Breakfast at hotel,
Drive to the town Madaba, where you find the famous mosaic 6th century map of Palestine in St. George’s church. After that continue to Mt. Nebo, the place where Prophet Moses gazed at the promised land before he died and alleged to be his burial place. You visit an old monastery; overlook the Jordan Valley, Dead Sea and parts of Israel. Afterward we drive to the lowest point on earth, the Dead Sea, where you have leisure time and the chance to float on water. Overnight Amman hotel.

Day 07:            Amman
Breakfast at hotel,

After breakfast transfer to Amman for departure
