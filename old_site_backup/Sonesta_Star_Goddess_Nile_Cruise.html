Sonesta Star Goddess Nile Cruise

The new Sonesta Star Goddess Nile Cruise Ship sails on the Nile River for three-, four- and seven-night cruises between Aswan and Luxor. All of the ship's 33 units are spacious suites, each with a private balcony and named after famous composers, giving it a touch of elegance and sophistication.

After guided tours of ancient sites, guests relax in the ship's swimming pool, enjoy a massage in the full-service spa or run on the ship's jogging track. Fine dining awaits in the restaurant and nightly entertainment caps off a memorable day.
