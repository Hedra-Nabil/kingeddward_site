Egypt Overland

Day 01:           
Arrive Cairo international airport, Meet and assist by our representative through customs and immigration formalities and transfer to Hotel for overnight

Day 02:           
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day guided tour to Giza Pyramids with Sphinx, and Sakkara, lunch during sightseeing then transfer back to the hotel.
Overnight at hotel in Cairo
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 03:           
Breakfast at the hotel, meet out tour guide to begin a full day city tour visiting: The Egyptian Museum, The Citadel, Mohamed Ali Mosque, the Old Coptic Churches in Old Cairo "Abou Serga and the Hanging Church and Khan El Khalili Bazaars, transfer to Cairo airport for flight to Luxor Meet and assist upon arrival at Luxor airport
     Transfer to Hotel, check in and overnight in Luxor  
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch.

Day 04:           
Visit Luxor west bank including Valley of the Kings, temple of Hatshepsut in Deir El Bahari, Colossi of Memnon
PM visit to Luxor temple and Karnak Temple
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 05:           
Transfer by air conditioned coach to Aswan, Visit to the Temple of Horus in Edfu and visit the temple of two gods, the crocodile God Sobek and Falcon God Haruris in Kom Ombo on the way, check in and overnight in Aswan  
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 06:           
Visit the high dam, unfinished obelisk, temple of Philae, felucca ride
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch

Day 07:
AM free and possibility to visit Abu Simbel
PM Check out and transfer to Aswan Airport flight to Cairo. Meet and assist upon arrival at Cairo airport
     Transfer to Hotel, check in and overnight in Cairo  
Meals: Breakfast and Lunch
     
Day 08:
Breakfast at hotel
Check out and transfer to Cairo Airport for final departure
